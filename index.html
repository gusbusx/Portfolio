About

Hello, I’m Augusto

I’m Augusto Segura, the founder & CEO of Gusify, a web design and development studio that builds premium, hand-coded websites for small businesses. I’m also a Computer Science student at Rutgers University–New Brunswick graduating in 2027, and a full-stack developer building polished web, mobile, software, and AI-powered products with Flutter, Dart, JavaScript, React, Next.js, HTML, and CSS.

I helped build ROOT, the first-place winner of the Fiserv × Rutgers Newark Hackathon, and I’m the founder of CloudDrop. Through Gusify I’ve shipped the Marler Enterprises and 2KD Hood Cleaning websites, and I also built StudyWeapon. I also work as a Computer Technician for Elizabeth Public Schools, where I configure, maintain, and troubleshoot devices supporting more than 2,000 students. I enjoy combining engineering, design, and problem-solving to create technology that is useful, intuitive, and reliable.
