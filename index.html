Hello, I’m Augusto

I’m Augusto Segura, a Computer Science student at Rutgers University–New Brunswick graduating in 2027. I’m a full-stack developer focused on building polished web, mobile, software, and AI-powered products using technologies such as Flutter, Dart, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

I helped build ROOT, the first-place winner of the Fiserv × Rutgers Newark Hackathon, and I’m the founder of CloudDrop. My other projects include StudyWeapon, the 2KD website, and the Marler Enterprises website. I also work as a Computer Technician for Elizabeth Public Schools, where I configure, maintain, and troubleshoot devices supporting more than 2,000 students. I enjoy combining engineering, design, and problem-solving to create technology that is useful, intuitive, and reliable.
